Our Mission

Breaking Cycles.
Building Futures.

The Comeback Project is a Texas-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to helping justice-involved individuals rebuild stable, productive lives. We serve Dallas and Ellis County by connecting people to workforce opportunities, skilled trades, mentorship, and the community resources they need to thrive after incarceration.

"Trades over trauma. Hope after release. Restoration and rebuilding."

What We Do

Programs & Services

🔧

Workforce Readiness

Resume building, interview prep, and connections to employers ready to hire returning citizens.

🏗️

Trades Apprenticeship

Connecting individuals to plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and construction — real careers with real futures.

🔓

Re-Entry Support

Guidance through the transition from incarceration — ID recovery, housing referrals, and more.

🤝

Mentorship

One-on-one mentoring, accountability, and community connection from people who understand the journey.

🎒

Community Drives

Hygiene drives and backpack drives for children and families impacted by incarceration.

🌐

Resource Connections

Referrals to clothing, food pantries, faith organizations, and partners throughout Dallas & Ellis County.
