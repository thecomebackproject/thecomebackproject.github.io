Why Partner With Us

Your Investment
Changes Lives

The Comeback Project is doing the hard work — meeting justice-involved individuals where they are and walking alongside them toward stability, employment, and restored dignity. When you partner with us, your investment goes directly into workforce training, trade apprenticeships, community drives, and mentorship programs that break cycles of recidivism and build stronger communities across Dallas and Ellis County.
